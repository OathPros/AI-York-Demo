# AI @ York public design

- Use York red, white, charcoal and cool grays for the public interface. Do not use brown, beige, taupe, gold or warm-neutral interface colours.
- Let all visible text fill its intended layout column and wrap naturally. Do not truncate, clamp, clip, add arbitrary text-specific max widths or force line breaks for visual balance.
- Use text labels for links and actions. Omit decorative arrows and instructional copy that repeats the controls or their labels.
- Keep the authoritative policy, Policy Studio, and administrative Studio intact when changing the public site.
